## Local fonts, October 2026

The original Google Fonts stylesheet has been replaced by bundled font files to support offline play. Cormorant Garamond and Manrope are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Licenses are included in assets/fonts/Cormorant-OFL.txt and Manrope-OFL.txt. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope. The unmodified files were downloaded from fonts.gstatic.com using the original family/weight selection in the first demo.
